# 🌌 Neon Typing Arcade 🦕

Welcome to **Neon Typing Arcade** — a collection of beautiful, fast-paced, web-based typing games built with modern vanilla HTML, CSS, and procedural audio!

---

## 🎮 The Games

### 1. 🦕 Dinosaur Adventure Typing (`index.html`)
A cute, modern classic! Type sentences to guide your pixel-art dinosaur across the field.
*   **Aesthetic**: Vibrant pixel-style dinosaur with walking animations and back spikes.
*   **Features**: Real-time WPM, Level, and Accuracy tracking.
*   **Audio**: Mechanical keyboard typing "clicks" and mistake buzzers synthesized procedurally using the Web Audio API.

### 2. 🌌 Neon Plane Typing (`plane-game.html`)
An arcade space-shooter style challenge! Words fall from the sky; lock on and type to blast them!
*   **Aesthetic**: Cyberpunk space grids, glowing starfields, and a sleek neon-themed player spaceship.
*   **Features**: 
    *   **Smooth Plane Tracking**: Your ship slides side-to-side dynamically, matching the horizontal position of your target!
    *   **Dual Audio Engine**: Custom procedural synth lasers/explosions + custom looping background music support.
    *   **Dynamic Difficulty**: Words fall faster and spawn more frequently as your score grows.
*   **Health System**: You have 3 hull points (lives). Don't let falling debris cross your defensive line!

---

## 🔊 Audio & Music Setup

To enable the background music loop in either game:
1.  Download any high-quality free looping chiptune or retro track (like those in OpenGameArt or Freesound).
2.  Save it into the project root directory as **`bgm.ogg`**.
3.  Launch either game, click the **🎵 Music: Off** button to toggle it on, and enjoy!

---

## 🚀 How to Play

No installation or build tools required!
1.  Clone this repository or download the ZIP.
2.  Double-click **`index.html`** (Dino Game) or **`plane-game.html`** (Neon Plane) to open them in any modern web browser.
3.  Type, blast, and achieve a high score!
